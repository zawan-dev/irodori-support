Privacy Policy

Irodori — Color Block Puzzle · Last updated: September 29, 2026

Irodori (“the App”) is developed by zawan-dev. We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs, and it does not require an account.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, statistics and settings are saved locally on your device (UserDefaults) so you can continue playing. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the App.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Apple Game Center, your scores are submitted to Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we do not receive your personal information.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
